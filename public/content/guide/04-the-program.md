# The Programme — Phases, Weeks & Gates

> [!NOTE]
> **In short**
> - Your clearance **gates** unlock the eight phases, not the calendar. Until a phase's gate is met, the current phase repeats its main weeks, deload included, and your loads keep climbing.
> - It's written for an experienced lifter coming back from an injury layoff: one Re-entry Week to set up every machine one-handed and find your loads, then full sets from programme week 2.
> - Until grip clearance your injured arm does nothing. Phases 0–1 train legs, engine, arm-free core and balance: at most six exercises a session, about 10–14 hard sets a week for quads and glutes and 8–10 for hamstrings and calves.
> - Every week has the same rhythm (three strength days, two engine days, a long walk and a rest day), and a deload comes every 5–6 weeks.
> - Short on time or gym closed? Two back-up sessions keep the habit going: never miss twice.

This is your map: what each phase is for, when it starts and what each clearance allows. The hub shows every session in detail. The reasons are in [Training Principles](03-training-principles.md); the arm-safe versions of each lift are in [Strength Technique](05-strength-technique.md).

## How the programme works

### Gates: your clearances

Each gate is a clearance from your fracture clinic or physio. Record it on the **Your arm** page the day you get it. Gates are cumulative: a higher clearance counts as all the lower ones.

| Gate | What it means | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Now** | Available now | Phases 0–1: legs, engine, arm-free core, balance next to a support |
| **Good arm OK** | You may train your uninjured arm | The seated good-arm add-on (Mon/Wed/Fri, Phases 0–1); grip readings for your uninjured hand; loading leg-press plates of 10 kg or less with your uninjured hand |
| **Light use / rehab started** | Light use of the injured arm, and/or physio rehab has started | Your physio's daily slot; a light day pack on walks; the couch stretch. It also unlocks everything under Good arm OK, so set it only if the clinic said yes to training your uninjured arm too |
| **Grip cleared** | Gripping and holding light to moderate weights; still no weight through the wrist, hanging or impact | Phase 2a (machines and cables), then Phases 2–3 (free weights); carries by the grip ladder |
| **Full clearance** | Heavy loading, carrying, hanging, weight on your hands, impact | Phase 4a (the bridge), then Phases 4–5 |

> [!WARNING]
> Only record a clearance the clinic or physio has actually given you. If they're more specific ("light gripping, nothing over 5 kg"), follow them and stay at the lower gate. Their advice always overrides this programme.

### Two rules at every gate

**The arm pain rule:** follow your physio's limit. If you weren't given one, keep pain at 2/10 or below during the exercise and back to your usual level by the next morning. Never train through pain that's increasing.

**The grip ladder:** your injured hand's grip as a percentage of your uninjured hand's. Every rung needs a reading your physio took, or their OK to move up.

| Injured-hand grip | What it allows | Also needs |
|---|---|---|
| At least 70% | Light holds and carries: suitcase carries, light farmer's carries, plate pinches, poles | Grip cleared |
| At least 80% | Moderate farmer's carries | Grip cleared |
| At least 90% | Heavy carries and hanging | Full clearance |

### How the hub schedules phases

- Phases run back to back from your **programme start date**, always a Monday (if you don't set one, the hub uses **the next Monday**).
- If a phase's gate isn't met when its turn comes, it waits and the current phase **repeats** its main weeks.
- Once the gate is met, the next phase starts on the **first Monday on or after** the later of your clearance date and the end of the current phase. Phase 1 can end after 4 weeks; the others run their full length.

**Two examples:**
- **Gripping cleared early**, on the Thursday of programme week 4: Phase 2a starts on **the Monday of programme week 6**.
- **Gripping cleared late**, on the Wednesday of programme week 12: from programme week 10 the hub repeats Phase 1 weeks 4–8, the week-5 deload included, and Phase 2a starts on **the Monday of programme week 13**.

### Week labels

Each week's settings and note appear at the top of the session:
- **Re-entry and intro weeks** cap your sets (3 in Re-entry Week, 2 in the first week of Phases 2a and 4a) and set the reps in reserve.
- **Primer weeks** cap the effort: Phase 1's bike efforts show Zone 3 until week 6, and Phase 2a's first 4 × 4 shows Zone 4.
- **Deload weeks** halve your sets and interval rounds, keep every zone at Zone 2 and shrink circuits to 5–6 easy minutes. Loads hold: "Hold: same load, RIR 3–4". They come every 5–6 weeks: Phase 1 week 5, Phase 2 week 3, Phase 3 weeks 3 and 9, Phase 4 weeks 5, 10 and 15, and Phase 5 week 5. A 2-set intro week at a phase change counts as a lighter week.

### Your weekly rhythm

| | Phases 0–3 and 4a | Phases 4–5 |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Strength A | **Rest** |
| Tue | Hiking engine (Zone 2) | Strength A |
| Wed | Strength B | Engine: intervals + Zone 2 |
| Thu | Engine: intervals + Zone 2 | Strength B |
| Fri | Strength C (+ circuit) | Strength C (legs kept fresh) |
| Sat | Long walk (light pack from Light use) | Long hike with a loaded pack |
| Sun | **Rest** (easy stroll + mobility) | Back-to-back hike |

Weekday sessions fit a 60–90-minute slot, warm-up included. Until full clearance, each strength session needs only one trip to the floor besides the warm-up's. Until Phase 4 the long walk is capped at 80 minutes; on a free weekend you may walk longer on good paths (log it on **Hikes**).

### Timeline if every gate arrives on time

A planning assumption, not a prediction: fracture healing varies a lot, and a late gate shifts everything after it.

### Fasting periods

If you fast for a period (for example Ramadan, another religious fast or a planned fast), add the dates in **Settings → Fasting periods**. On those days the hub applies easy-day mode automatically: half the sets, Zone 2 only, no intervals, loads held, no tests due. Train after you've broken your fast if you can, stop at any dizziness or light-headedness, and start no new lifts until the period ends. Phase dates don't move. Food and drink around a fast: [Nutrition](14-nutrition.md).

## Common gym gaps and substitutes

The plan is written for a typical commercial gym. Where kit is often missing, it already has a substitute:

| If your gym has… | What the plan does |
|---|---|
| A plate-loaded 45° leg press with safety catches | **Dead start:** staff set the bottom stops once; the catches stay released and the sled rests on the stops |
| No sled belt or harness | **Tempo squats** replace the sled drag in the circuits |
| No ankle straps | **Side-lying hip abduction** and the **adductor-squeeze bridge** |
| No plyo boxes | An **aerobic step (15–25 cm)** or the bottom stair for every step exercise until full clearance, never a bench |

## Phase 0 — Re-entry Week (1 week)

**Goal.** Six mornings to come back after the layoff: set up each machine one-handed, get the leg press's stops set, find your working loads and your Zone 2 (60–70% of your max heart rate: about 112–131 bpm for the 30-year-old example profile; Settings shows yours), and take your baseline tests. You know the lifts; what's new is doing them without your injured arm.

Re-entry Week runs the Phase 1 sessions below on Mon and Wed, capped at 3 sets (the hub shows RIR 3–4), and the same Sunday rest day. The other days are shorter re-entry versions:
- **Tue and Thu:** Hiking Engine — Zone 2 (re-entry week), 45 min
- **Fri:** Strength C — Single-Leg & Circuit (re-entry week), 60 min, with 2 easy circuit rounds
- **Sat:** Long Walk — Zone 2 (re-entry week), 50 min

**Finding your loads.** On every exercise with 3 or more sets: 2 sets at 3–4 reps in reserve, then a calibration set at 2 in reserve; log the RIR you actually had. Start around a load you could do for 15, then let double progression find your level within 2 sessions. Monday's leg-press calibration set is your re-entry baseline.

**Your Re-entry Week checklist** (the hub's "Ready to move on when"):

- PAR-Q+ done before your first session (a 'yes' to the bone question is expected; do what it advises)
- Staff have set the leg press's bottom stops for your dead start (hole number in your notes)
- You can set up every Phase 1 machine without using your injured arm
- A working load for every Phase 1 lift, from your calibration sets
- Baseline tests logged on days 1–3; Monday's leg-press calibration set is your re-entry baseline
- At least 5 of 6 sessions done, with arm pain no worse than before you started

> [!SAFETY]
> The first two weeks after a long layoff are when unaccustomed volume bites. Soreness for a day or two is normal. Dark (cola-coloured) urine with severe muscle pain or swelling after training is not: get same-day medical advice (possible rhabdomyolysis).

## Phase 1 — Foundation (8 weeks, minimum 4; repeats weeks 4–8)

**Goal.** Rebuild the legs, engine and core that long hikes need, with your injured arm doing nothing. Full sets from week 1: about 12 hard sets a week for quads, 13 for glutes, 9 for hamstrings and 8 for calves, every muscle trained twice, and Strength C ends with a 10-minute arm-free circuit. With muscle memory on your side, expect loads to climb quickly.

| Day | Session | Min | What's in it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Strength A — Squat & Knee | 70 | Leg press, rear-foot-elevated split squat, Patrick step, single-leg calf raise, tibialis raise, dead bug |
| Tue | Hiking Engine — Zone 2 | 70 | Stair climber, incline walk, mobility |
| Wed | Strength B — Hinge & Posterior Chain | 65 | Hamstring curl, single-leg RDL, bent-knee calf raise, side-lying hip abduction, single-leg hip thrust, tuck hollow hold |
| Thu | Engine — Bike Intervals + Zone 2 | 60 | Bike 6 × 30 s hard, incline walk, mobility |
| Fri | Strength C — Single-Leg & Circuit | 60 | Single-leg press, leg extension, hamstring curl, adductor-squeeze bridge, 10-min circuit (tempo squat and step-up) |
| Sat | Long Walk — Zone 2 (good paths) | 90 | Walk, mobility |
| Sun | Rest Day — Easy Walk & Mobility | 40 | Walk, mobility |

**Week by week: one change at a time:**

| Week | Programme week | Label | The one change | What the hub sets |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | Full sets | **Full sets** (3 of every main lift) | RIR 2–3 on everything, intervals capped at Zone 3 |
| 2 | 3 | Build | — | RIR 2–3 on everything, intervals capped at Zone 3 |
| 3 | 4 | Steeper incline | **Incline up to 6–12%** | RIR 2–3 on everything, intervals capped at Zone 3 |
| 4 | 5 | Machines to RIR 1–2 | **Machine leg work to RIR 1–2** | Intervals capped at Zone 3 |
| 5 | 6 | Deload | **Deload** | Half the sets and interval rounds, RIR 3–4 on everything, everything in Zone 2, 5-min easy circuit |
| 6 | 7 | Hard efforts | **Hard bike efforts (8/10)** | No caps |
| 7 | 8 | — | — | No caps |
| 8 | 9 | Consolidate & check the gate | Retests; gate check | No caps |

Machine leg work means the leg press, leg extension and hamstring curl. The single-leg press (one leg can fail suddenly) and everything free-standing (a wobble near failure is a fall risk) stay at 2–3 reps in reserve. Gym shut for a holiday? Use the **Gym closed?** session below. While you wait for grip clearance, the repeat of weeks 4–8 brings the deload round every 5 weeks.

**Targets:** 150 minutes of Zone 2 a week, 3 strength sessions, 8,000 steps a day on average, a long session of 80 minutes.

**Ready to move on when:**

- Leg press: estimated 1RM (Progress) at least 30% above your Re-entry Week calibration set
- Stair climber: 25 minutes continuous in Zone 2
- 80-minute brisk walk on good paths with no aches the next day
- Single-leg balance at least 45 s each side, eyes open, next to a support
- Your physio has cleared gripping and holding weights: this is what actually unlocks Phase 2a

## Phase 2a — Rebuild: Machines First (2 weeks)

**Goal.** Your first two weeks with both hands: upper-body work on machines and cables only, at a weight you could lift 20 times, while your legs keep progressing. Week 1 is 2 sets of everything; the first 4 × 4 is capped at Zone 4 (7/10).

|---|---|---|---|

| Fri | Rebuild C — Single-Leg, Row & Circuit (machines first) | 85 | Rear-foot-elevated split squat, single-leg press, hamstring curl, row machine, cable woodchop, adductor-squeeze bridge, 12-min circuit (tempo squat, lateral step-up, step-down, bent-knee calf raise and tibialis raise) |
| Sat | Long Walk with a Light Pack (Rebuild) | 90 | Light-pack walk, mobility |
| Sun | Rest Day — Easy Walk & Mobility | 40 | Walk, mobility |

**Ready to move on when:**

- Two weeks of machine and cable work within the arm pain rule
- Physio happy for you to start light dumbbells

## Phase 2 — Rebuild (6 weeks)

**Goal.** Free weights come back, carefully: a light goblet squat, Pavel's two-handed kettlebell deadlift, barbell hip thrusts and light dumbbells. **Suitcase carries begin only at the 70% rung, with your physio's OK.** Any dumbbell in single-leg work goes in your injured hand, with your uninjured hand on the rack. Week 1 is light (fasting? keep to the machines until it ends); week 3 is a deload.

| Day | Session | Min | What's in it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Full Body A — Squat & Push (Rebuild) | 85 | Leg press, goblet squat, chest press, lat pulldown, single-leg calf raise, Pallof press, face pull |
| Tue | Hiking Engine — Zone 2 (Rebuild) | 70 | Stair climber, incline walk, mobility |
| Wed | Full Body B — Hinge, Pull & Carry (Rebuild) | 85 | Kettlebell deadlift, hamstring curl, cable row, dumbbell shoulder press, suitcase carry, tibialis raise, barbell hip thrust, McGill curl-up |
| Thu | VO2max — 4 × 4 min (bike) | 70 | Bike 4 × 4 min hard, incline walk, mobility |
| Fri | Full Body C — Single-Leg, Push/Pull & Circuit (Rebuild) | 85 | Rear-foot-elevated split squat, single-leg press, hamstring curl, dumbbell bench press, chest-supported row, adductor-squeeze bridge, 12-min circuit (tempo squat, suitcase carry, step-up, bent-knee calf raise and cable woodchop) |
| Sat | Long Walk with a Light Pack (Rebuild) | 90 | Light-pack walk, mobility |
| Sun | Rest Day — Easy Walk & Mobility | 40 | Walk, mobility |

**Ready to move on when:**

- Six weeks of free-weight work within the arm pain rule
- Injured-hand grip at least 70% of the good hand, measured by your physio
- Suitcase carry 4 × 30 s each side with 10 kg or more, pain-free
- Zone 2 at least 150 minutes a week for 4 weeks

## Phase 3 — Build (12 weeks; repeats weeks 7–12)

**Goal.** Your main muscle-building block: Smith squats, barbell RDLs and hip thrusts, about 13–16 hard sets a week for quads and glutes and 6–9 for chest, back and shoulders, farmer's carries by the ladder, a 5 × 4 VO2max session (4 × 4 in week 1) and pack walks with more climbing. Weeks 3 and 9 are deloads; week 12 brings the full-clearance question for your clinic.

| Day | Session | Min | What's in it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Full Body A — Squat & Push (Build) | 85 | Smith squat, leg press, dumbbell bench press, lat pulldown, single-leg calf raise, cable woodchop |
| Tue | Hiking Engine — Zone 2 with Pack (Build) | 75 | Stair climber, light-pack walk, mobility |
| Wed | Full Body B — Hinge, Pull & Carry (Build) | 85 | Barbell RDL, barbell hip thrust, hamstring curl, cable row, dumbbell shoulder press, farmer's carry, plate pinch |
| Thu | VO2max — 5 × 4 min (bike) | 80 | Bike 5 × 4 min hard, incline walk, mobility |
| Fri | Full Body C — Single-Leg, Arms & Circuit (Build) | 85 | Bulgarian split squat, loaded step-up, chest press, row machine, triceps pushdown, dumbbell curl, 12-min circuit (tempo squat, sled rope pull, suitcase carry, lateral step-up and tibialis raise) |
| Sat | Long Walk with a Pack — More Climbing (Build) | 90 | Light-pack walk, mobility |
| Sun | Rest Day — Easy Walk & Mobility | 40 | Walk, mobility |

**Ready to move on when:**

- Injured-hand grip at least 80% of the good hand, measured by your physio (moderate farmer's carries)
- Farmer's carry 4 × 30 s with about 15% of body weight per hand, within the arm pain rule
- Body-weight trend inside your weekly band from chapter 14 (the hub's fortnightly verdict) with the waist roughly stable
- Full clearance from the fracture clinic — this is what unlocks Phase 4a

## Phases 4a–5 — Bridge, Forge and Mission (after full clearance)

These start only with full clearance; the hub shows every session when you get there. From Phase 4, Monday is the rest day so both weekend days can be hiking days. Farmer's-carry goals follow the standards in [Farmer Strength](09-farmer-strength.md).

| Phase | Weeks | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 4a — Bridge to Forge | 4 | Brings each full-clearance skill in one step at a time: hangs, incline push-ups, swings, sandbags, sled pushes, free-bar squats and rougher terrain |
| Phase 4 — Forge | 16 | Barbell strength, Simple & Sinister swings and get-ups, odd-object circuits, heavy carries and back-to-back weekend hikes; Monday becomes the rest day |
| Phase 5 — Mission | 10 | A repeating 10-week loop that peaks for a big hike: lower-volume lifting, growing weekends, a taper week and a Mission week |

Phase 4 deloads fall in weeks 5, 10 and 15, and week 16 is a benchmark week. Phase 5 has a deload in week 5, then a taper (week 9) and a Mission week (week 10). If every gate arrives on time, the first Mission week is programme week 59; if it falls in winter, pick a route that fits the daylight ([Hiking Skills, Kit & Mountain Safety](12-hiking-skills-and-kit.md)).

## Progression in one paragraph

Double progression on almost everything: work up the rep range at the same load, then add the smallest step and start again at the bottom. Bodyweight moves add reps, then tempo or a pause, then a harder version or your day pack; holds and carries add 5–10 s, then load. Deload weeks, AMBER days and fasting periods hold loads. The full rules, and why muscle memory speeds up the first months: [Training Principles](03-training-principles.md).

## Testing

This chapter sets the test timing. Baselines are spread over days 1–3 of Re-entry Week, then every 4 weeks on a GREEN morning, after the bike warm-up; **Progress → Tests** shows what's due. Nothing is due during a fasting period, and none of the tests is a one-rep max.

| Test | Open | First due |
|---|---|---|
| 30-second sit-to-stand | Now | Re-entry Week day 1 (Mon) |
| Knee-to-wall ankle test (weaker side) | Now | Re-entry Week day 1 (Mon) |
| Single-leg balance, eyes open (weaker side) | Now | Re-entry Week day 1 (Mon) |
| 1-mile (1.6 km) brisk walk test | Now | Re-entry Week day 2 (Tue) |
| Single-leg heel raise to fatigue (weaker side) | Now | Re-entry Week day 3 (Wed) |
| Hollow hold, knees tucked (max hold) | Now | Re-entry Week day 3 (Wed) |
| Farmer's carry: time with 25% of body weight per hand | Full clearance | Phase 4a week 4 |
| Dead hang | Full clearance | Phase 4a week 4 |

Not tests, because you log them anyway:
- **Leg press:** your re-entry baseline is your best Re-entry Week set (Monday's calibration set at RIR 2), as an estimated 1RM on Progress. The target is **+30%** by the end of Phase 1.
- **Grip:** the **Your arm** page, measured by your physio or with their OK. **Weight, resting heart rate, waist and thigh:** your check-in and Progress → Measurements.

## When life happens

- **Missed a day:** don't double up. A missed strength day can replace the next engine day if legs aren't trained hard two days running.
- **Time off:** up to a week, repeat your last loads; one to two weeks, drop about 10% and do 2 sets for a week; longer, drop about 20% and start with a deload week.
- **Ill:** above the neck only, an easy Zone 2 walk at most. Fever, chest symptoms, aches or a stomach bug: rest, then restart 24–48 hours after symptoms clear with a deload-style week.
- **Bad weather:** ice, frost, wet leaves, mud or darkness move the walk to the treadmill until full clearance.
- **RED morning** (any arm red flag, or arm pain 7/10 or more): stop. No training today, including your physio exercises, until you've been advised; contact your fracture clinic today, or NHS 111 / 999 as the red-flag guidance says.
- **AMBER morning:** half the sets, Zone 2 only, no intervals, loads held. If arm pain keeps rising, or you're unsure, call your fracture clinic today. The full rule is in [Training Principles](03-training-principles.md).

## Your actions this week

- **Check your start date** in Settings (if you haven't set one, it's the next Monday).
- **Before Monday:** PAR-Q+ done, and the leg press's bottom stops set by staff (note the hole).
- **Take your baseline tests on days 1–3.**
- **Do the six Re-entry Week sessions:** 2 sets at RIR 3–4 plus a calibration set at RIR 2 on every main lift, noting every machine setting.
- **Plan for holidays and gym closures:** the **Gym closed?** session or the long walk.
